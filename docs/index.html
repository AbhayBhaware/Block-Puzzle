How it works

Simple rules.
Endless depth.

  1. 1
    Pick a piece

    Three block pieces are shown at the bottom. Choose which to place next.

  2. 2
    Drop it on the board

    Drag the piece to any valid position on the 8×8 grid.

  3. 3
    Clear lines & score

    Complete full rows or columns — they vanish and you score points.

  4. 4
    Survive as long as possible

    The game ends when no more pieces can be placed. Beat your best.

💡 Pro Tip

Clear multiple lines at once for a combo bonus. Plan your placements two or three moves ahead.

🪙 Use your coins wisely

Revives cost 200 coins. Save them for when you're having your best-ever run.
