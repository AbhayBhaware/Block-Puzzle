Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to Block Blast Ify, developed by 11:11 Studio ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application (the "App") available on Google Play.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect

Block Blast Ify is designed with your privacy in mind. Here is what the App does and does not collect:

  • No personal data collected directly: We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.
  • Local game data: Your high score, coin balance, and game preferences (music/sound on/off) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • Advertising data: The App uses Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads. See Section 4 for details.

3. How We Use Information

The limited data associated with the App is used solely to:

  • Save your game progress (score, coins, settings) on your local device.
  • Serve advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free availability of the App.
  • Improve gameplay experience in future updates.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob is a Google service and may collect and process the following data to serve ads:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Diagnostic information

This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, which you can review at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Our AdMob Publisher ID is: pub-9423620095795922

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as described in Section 4 (Google AdMob). We do not have access to any data that AdMob collects on our behalf.

6. Children's Privacy

Block Blast Ify is a casual puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

7. Security

Game data (score, coins, preferences) is stored only on your local device and is not transmitted to our servers. We implement reasonable safeguards, but no method of data storage is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access or request deletion of any personal data associated with you.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising by resetting or limiting your Android Advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Uninstall the App at any time — all local data will be removed from your device.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
