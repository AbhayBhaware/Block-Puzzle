Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

App: Block Blastify · Developer: 11:11 Studio · Updated: September 28, 2024

Block Blastify is a simple, free puzzle game. We've kept data collection minimal by design. This policy tells you exactly what's involved.

Introduction

Welcome to Block Blastify, developed by 11:11 Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use our Android app and how it is handled.

By downloading and playing Block Blastify, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information directly. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier.

How We Use Data

  • To save your game progress (score, coins, preferences) locally on your device.
  • To serve ads via Google AdMob, which keeps the game free for everyone.
  • To improve app stability and fix bugs in future updates.

Google AdMob

Block Blastify uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and may collect the following to deliver and personalise ads:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device model and OS version
  • App usage and interaction events

To opt out of personalised ads, reset your Advertising ID on your Android device: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. The only external data processing occurs through Google AdMob as described in Section 4 above, which we do not control directly.

Children's Privacy

Block Blastify is a casual puzzle game suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through our app, contact us via the Google Play listing and we will promptly remove it.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Opt out of personalised ads: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or limit Ad ID
  • Delete game data: Settings → Apps → Block Blastify → Clear Data
  • Remove all data: Uninstall the app — all local data is deleted automatically
  • Data deletion request: Contact us via the Google Play Store listing page

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted here with a revised date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the app's Google Play listing.
