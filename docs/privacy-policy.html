Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2024

Introduction

Block Blastify is developed by 11:11 Studio ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our Android app available on Google Play. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

What We Collect

We collect very little. Here is the full picture:

  • No personal data directly: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.
  • Local game data: Your high score, coin balance, and settings (music/sound) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • Advertising data via AdMob: The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. See the Advertising section below.

How We Use Data

  • To save your game progress locally on your device.
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob, keeping the app free.
  • To improve gameplay in future updates.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-9423620095795922) to serve ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Diagnostic information

This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except through AdMob as described above. We have no access to data AdMob collects.

Children's Privacy

Block Blastify is a casual game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

Your Rights

  • Reset or limit your Android Advertising ID via Settings → Google → Ads to opt out of personalized ads.
  • Uninstall the app at any time — all local data is removed automatically.
  • Contact us to request deletion of any data associated with you.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected here with an updated date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
