Privacy Policy

App: Block Blastify  ·  Developer: 11:11 Studio  ·  Last updated: September 28, 2024

We built Block Blastify as a free, simple puzzle game. This policy explains clearly what data is involved when you play, and what happens with it. We believe in full transparency.

At a glance

✅ No personal data collected
✅ Game data stays on your device
✅ No accounts or sign-up
⚠️ Ads shown via Google AdMob
✅ No data sold to third parties
✅ Safe for all ages
01

Introduction

Welcome to Block Blastify, a free Android block puzzle game developed by 11:11 Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use our app and how it is handled.

By downloading and playing Block Blastify, you agree to the practices described here. If you disagree with any part, please uninstall the app.

02

Information We Collect

Directly collected by us — none. We do not ask for or store your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.

High Score
Stored locally on your device only. Never transmitted.
Coin Balance
Stored locally on your device only. Never transmitted.
Settings (music/sound)
Stored locally on your device only. Never transmitted.
Ad-related data
Collected by Google AdMob to serve ads. See Section 04.
03

How We Use Information

  • To save your game progress (score, coins, preferences) on your local device.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob, which keeps the game free for everyone.
  • To analyse crash reports (if any) to improve app stability in future updates.
04

Advertising — Google AdMob

Block Blastify uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and may collect the following to serve ads:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID)
  • IP address and coarse location
  • Device model and operating system version
  • App usage and interaction events
Our AdMob Publisher ID: pub-9423620095795922

This data collection is governed entirely by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time by resetting your Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID.

05

Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. The only data sharing that occurs is through Google AdMob (described in Section 04), which we do not control directly.

06

Children's Privacy

Block Blastify is a casual puzzle game suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has somehow provided personal data through our app, please contact us via the Google Play listing and we will take immediate steps to remove it.

07

Data Security

All game data (score, coins, settings) is stored exclusively on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data is never uploaded to any server.

No method of storage is 100% secure, but since your data never leaves your device, the risk surface is minimal.

08

Your Rights & Choices

  • Opt out of personalised ads: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or limit Ad ID.
  • Delete your game data: Settings → Apps → Block Blastify → Clear Data.
  • Uninstall at any time: All local data is deleted automatically.
  • Data deletion request: Contact us via the Play Store listing page.
09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
